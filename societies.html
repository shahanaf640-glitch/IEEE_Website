Societies & Affinity Groups

Join communities aligned with your interests

IEEE operates several technical societies and affinity groups. Within IEEE SB STM, we support active chapters in multiple disciplines, providing specialized knowledge, networking, and professional development opportunities.

Our Active Societies

💻

Computer Society (CS)

Focus: Computing and Software Engineering

Programming workshops, coding competitions, software development projects, and talks on emerging technologies like AI and machine learning.

  • Competitive programming contests
  • Web and app development workshops
  • Tech talks from industry experts
  • Collaborative coding projects
👩‍💼

Women in Engineering (WIE)

Focus: Empowering women in technology

Mentorship programs, leadership training, and initiatives to support and celebrate women in engineering and technology careers.

  • Mentorship matching
  • Leadership workshops
  • Career guidance and networking
  • Community outreach programs
⚡

Power & Energy Society (PES)

Focus: Power Systems and Energy

Technical talks and projects focused on power systems, renewable energy, smart grids, and sustainable energy solutions.

  • Power systems seminar series
  • Renewable energy projects
  • Smart grid technology talks
  • Industry collaboration
🤖

Robotics & Automation (RAS)

Focus: Robotics and Autonomous Systems

Hands-on robotics projects, automation workshops, building robots, and competing in national robotics competitions.

  • ROV and drone projects
  • Robot building workshops
  • Robotics competitions
  • Automation technology talks
📡

Signal Processing & Communications

Focus: Signal Processing, Communications

Workshops on digital signal processing, telecommunications, wireless communications, and modern communication technologies.

  • Signal processing tutorials
  • 5G and wireless tech talks
  • Communication system projects
  • Emerging tech seminars
🎓

Student Branch

Focus: Overall member engagement

General membership, networking events, career development, mentorship, and opportunities to explore multiple technical disciplines.

  • Networking events
  • Career fairs
  • General meetings
  • Social and community events

Get Involved

Join a Society

As an IEEE member, you can join one or multiple societies aligned with your interests and career goals. Each society offers specialized knowledge and targeted networking within your discipline.

Most society activities are open to all IEEE members at no additional cost.

Volunteer & Lead

Interested in organizing society activities? Volunteer opportunities are available for event planning, mentoring, and leadership roles.

Contact the IEEE SB STM leadership team or reach out to individual society coordinators to get involved.
