Frequently Asked Questions

How do I become a member of IEEE SB STM?

First, you need to become an IEEE member by joining at ieee.org/membership/join. Then, select IEEE Student Branch at St. Thomas College, Kannur as your local branch during the signup process.

What are the membership fees?

IEEE Student membership has special discounted rates. The exact fee varies based on membership type and your location. Visit ieee.org/membership for current pricing.

When are events typically held?

Events are held throughout the academic year, typically on weekends and after college hours. Subscribe to our newsletter or follow Instagram for the latest event announcements.

Can I attend events without being a member?

Most of our events are open to all students, but certain activities may be reserved for members. Check the event details or contact us for specific information.

How can I volunteer or lead activities?

Contact us through this form or email ieeesb@stthomaskannur.ac.in to inquire about volunteer opportunities. Leadership roles are elected annually.

How do I get involved in a society?

Visit the Societies page to learn about each society. Reach out to us to know how to join the societies you're interested in.
