How to Join IEEE SB STM

1

Visit IEEE Membership

Go to ieee.org/membership/join to explore options and pricing.

2

Create Your Account

Fill out the membership application with your details and academic/professional information.

3

Select Student Membership

Choose "Student Member" if you're a full-time student, or another category matching your status.

4

Choose IEEE SB STM

When asked to select your local branch, choose IEEE Student Branch at St. Thomas College, Kannur.

5

Complete Payment

Pay the membership fee securely. Student rates are often discounted compared to professional membership.

6

Start Engaging

Access member resources, attend IEEE SB STM events, and connect with the community!
