About IEEE

The world's largest technical professional organization

What is IEEE?

IEEE is the world's largest technical professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE by Numbers

  • 400,000+ Members worldwide
  • 160+ Countries represented
  • 1,900+ Local sections
  • 430+ Student branches
  • 300+ Standards published

Vision & Mission

Vision Statement

IEEE will be essential to the global technical community and to technical professionals everywhere, and be universally recognized for the contributions of technology and of technical professionals in improving global conditions.

Mission Statement

IEEE's core purpose is to foster technological innovation and excellence for the benefit of humanity.

IEEE Student Programs

Student Membership

IEEE Student membership provides access to:

  • IEEE publications and Xplore digital library
  • Technical conferences and workshops
  • Professional networking opportunities
  • Career development resources
  • Leadership and volunteer opportunities

Student Branches

IEEE operates over 430 student branches worldwide, including IEEE SB STM. Student branches:

  • Organize local events and workshops
  • Connect members with professionals
  • Build technical and leadership skills
  • Support community outreach
  • Prepare students for professional careers
